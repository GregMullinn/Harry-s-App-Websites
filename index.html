Making a spot colour & applying overprint

What this tool can and can't tell you
  • Checks are based on the PDF's page boxes and object structure (TrimBox / BleedBox geometry, Separation & DeviceN colour spaces, ExtGState overprint flags) plus a scan of the page content streams.
  • It confirms that boxes and colours are set correctly. It does not render the artwork, so it can't confirm that your image data actually fills the bleed, or visually verify a knockout. Always eyeball the file in Overprint Preview too.
  • Page rotation and /UserUnit scaling are ignored in the crop-mark geometry check.
  • Encrypted / password-protected PDFs may give unreliable results.
